## 3. PLAN-1 — the dependency

**Identifier.** `C-S4-3`, step ref `STAGE4-S4-3`, band `filesystem`, evidence
case `S4-3`, `blocks_mutation_ids=()`. I did not use a bare number. `C-9` is
already a hypothetical conflict id in `test_concrete_plan.py`, and bare `C-n`
already collides between the classifier's C-1…C-4 and the concrete plan's
C-1…C-8.

**What it requires**, from `design_requires`, all four:

1. the reviewed deployed `freedom-sheet-writer.service` and its allowed drop-in
   policy;
2. a capture vector requesting every property in
   `unit_sandbox.COMPARED_PROPERTIES` exactly once;
3. the canonical `ReadWritePaths=` substitution,
   `unit_sandbox.CANONICAL_PROBE_PATH`; and
4. a reviewed producer and binding for `unit_sandbox.SystemdIdentity`.

**How it is decided.** `s4_3_dependency_gaps(producer, requested_properties=…,
substituted_path=…)` returns every unmet requirement in a fixed order.
Requirements 2 and 3 are read from the plan's own vectors, never from the
producer, because condition 3 forbids taking the path from a caller. Today it
returns three gaps: no deployed unit, 2 of 12 properties, and no identity
producer. Requirement 3 is already met for the approved target. The gap text is
rendered into `why_not_a_vector`.

> *Update 2026-09-23, C-P5.0-R5-R3:* **superseded as to requirements 1 and 4.** Codex's review
> (P5.0-R5-R2-PLAN-1) found that `SandboxAttestationProducer("x", "y")` with a
> complete vector cleared both, so a label could stand in for the reviewed unit,
> the reviewed `SystemdIdentity` producer and their binding. R3 removes the
> class, `S4_3_PRODUCER` and the resolved branch. `s4_3_dependency_gaps` takes
> only the two plan-derived inputs and always reports requirements 1 and 4 unmet,
> and `_stage_four` declares C-S4-3 unconditionally. See the
> [R3 handback](phase-5-0-p5-r5-r3-s4-3-binding-and-invalidation-remediation-handback.md).

**Why the capture step no longer claims S4-3.**
`required_cases.check_case_coverage` refuses a case in more than one of
produced, unresolved and external. A step that cannot produce S4-3 should not
claim that it does. The step stays in the plan with the same argv, the same
expectations and the same step id (`B4-34`). This follows the existing
`R-B-UNIT` precedent of a `UNIT_DIRECTIVES` capture with no evidence case.

**Where it appears.** `plan.unresolved`, `unresolved_by_band("filesystem")`,
`conflicts()`, the rendered plan's §6 (`### STAGE4-S4-3 — C-S4-3
(filesystem)`), the manifest's `unresolved` list, and the dry-run summary line.

**A limit, stated rather than fixed.** S4-3 is **not** a
`required_cases.REQUIRED_CASES` row. That table holds only the
capability-matrix and Band-7 cases, and its docstring says it *"grows when a
reviewer adds a row"*. So `producer_mapping()` and the importer's
`unresolved_cases` do not list S4-3. The overlap refusal still applies to it,
and `is_executable` is what C-S4-3 gates. **Adding S4-3, or all Stage 1–4 probe
cases, as required cases is left to the reviewer.** Without that row, a future
edit that removed both the `UnresolvedStep` and the step attribution would not
be caught by `check_case_coverage`. The new tests do catch it.

> *Update 2026-09-23, C-P5.0-R5-R3:* S4-3 is now a `REQUIRED_CASES` row, alone. `check_case_coverage`
> refuses that deletion, and `producer_mapping()` and the manifest list S4-3 as
> blocked by C-S4-3.
