### Consumed status — RP-11 publication redesign accepted; implementation was unassigned — 2026-09-28

Peter Duscha instructed Codex to change the approach after I1-R2 exposed both
context-dependent `ENOENT` and a race between cleanup identity verification and
pathname unlink. The repository-only
