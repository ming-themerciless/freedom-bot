Discord Authentication Overview

Sign in using your Discord account to view your linked Freedom Blades characters, downtime records, and guild history.

  • Minimum Scopes: Requests identify and guilds.members.read only (ADR 0004).
  • Guild Membership Verification: Required server-side on session start.
  • Server-Side Sessions: Session token lives in server memory/database; opaque cookie carried by client.
