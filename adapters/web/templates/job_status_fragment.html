Every HTMX partial is also reachable as a full page (view-model contract §9.1), so this fragment is the whole of what R-43 renders inside its layout. Writing it twice would be two renderings of one contract, and the one that drifted would be the one nobody was looking at. **Polling honours `poll_after_seconds` and stops on a terminal state.** The interval is the server's N-22 floor, echoed in `Retry-After` as well, and `hx-trigger` is static template text — there is no `hx-on:` attribute anywhere, no inline handler and no client-built URL, because each of those is script and would force a CSP weakening. `hx-trigger` appears in exactly one place in this file, inside the live-state branch, so a terminal job's fragment contains the attribute nowhere at all rather than in a disabled form. **HTMX is an enhancement, never the mechanism.** Cancellation and confirmation are ordinary `POST` forms, the state is readable without a swap, and a nonterminal job carries a plain link that re-reads R-43.
